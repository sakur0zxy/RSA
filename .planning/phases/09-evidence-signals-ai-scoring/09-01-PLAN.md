## Must Haves

<must_haves>
<truth id="D-01">D-01: Phase 9 must support both single-paper scoring and campaign-item scoring, with single-paper scoring as the reusable core.</truth>
<truth id="D-02">D-02: `rsa score P001` is the smallest verifiable unit, so schema paths and helpers must center on `paper_id`.</truth>
<truth id="D-03">D-03: Campaign support must stay a summary/iteration layer and must not become the full Phase 11 review system.</truth>
<truth id="D-04">D-04: Single-paper scoring YAML must live at `01_literature/scores/P###_scoring.yaml`.</truth>
<truth id="D-05">D-05: Campaign scoring summaries must live at `01_literature/campaigns/C###_scoring_summary.yaml`.</truth>
<truth id="D-06">D-06: Phase 8 `agent_review_score_10` remains a reading-draft readiness score and is not reused as a Phase 9 score.</truth>
<truth id="D-07">D-07: Scoring YAML is staging/review machine-readable output; review packets are user-facing aids, not formal records.</truth>
<truth id="D-14">D-14: Main scoring scale is 0-10.</truth>
<truth id="D-15">D-15: The schema must contain separate `ai_relevance_score_10`, `ai_quality_score_10`, and `ai_read_priority_score_10` fields.</truth>
<truth id="D-16">D-16: Quality scoring must be backed by rubric subitems instead of a freeform single judgment.</truth>
<truth id="D-17">D-17: Chinese threshold semantics must be represented in generated status text and validation-friendly fields.</truth>
<truth id="D-18">D-18: `6 分以上建议通过` means staging recommendation only, not formal approval.</truth>
<truth id="D-25">D-25: Schema must support AI automatic judgment plus user supervision, traceability and later correction.</truth>
<truth id="D-26">D-26: Allowed AI review decisions are `recommend_pass`, `recommend_defer`, `needs_review`, and `blocked`.</truth>
<truth id="D-30">D-30: Human changes must preserve `human_review` and append-only `review_history` information.</truth>
<truth id="D-31">D-31: `recommend_pass` is not `approved`; formal approval remains outside Phase 9.</truth>
<truth id="D-36">D-36: User-facing output, errors and review text are Chinese-first; schema keys, flags and status enum values remain English.</truth>
</must_haves>

  - `scoring_path(config, paper_id)`;
  - `review_packet_path(config, paper_id)`;

  - `load_scoring_record(config, paper_id)`;

- `write_scoring_record` writes UTF-8 YAML with `allow_unicode=True` and does not sort keys.
</acceptance_criteria>
</task>

<threat_model>
- **Threat:** Users or later agents treat score YAML as formal academic truth. **Mitigation:** required `formal_record_policy_zh`, staging `ai_review_decision`, and no formal write command in Phase 9.
- **Threat:** Phase 9 silently overwrites user correction history. **Mitigation:** validation requires `human_review` and `review_history`; later review updates append history entries.
- **Threat:** Future campaign logic diverges from single-paper scoring. **Mitigation:** shared path/schema helpers and plan 09-03 requirement to call the same single-paper scoring function.
</threat_model>
