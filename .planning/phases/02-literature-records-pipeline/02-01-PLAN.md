<threat_model>
- T-02-01 Unconfirmed formal write: a candidate becomes formal metadata without human approval. Mitigate by requiring `--human-confirmed` and `--confirmed-by`, and by rejecting `human_confirmed: false` in formal validation.
- T-02-02 ID drift: failed writes consume or skip `P###`. Mitigate by validating inputs before allocating the next ID and by writing exactly one `metadata/P###.yaml` after validation passes.
- T-02-03 User-facing ambiguity: English keys are stable for tools but unclear to the user. Mitigate with Chinese field explanations in templates, CLI help, and success/error messages.
</threat_model>
