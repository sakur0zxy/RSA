## Residual Risk

- Some databases rely on localStorage, JS navigation or short-lived signed URLs rather than simple cookies. Those cases are correctly outside v1 automation and should be handled by future browser automation work.
- Users must ensure their custom provider URL and usage policy match their actual access rights; RSA records and enforces the configured boundary but cannot independently prove institutional license terms.
